Auto Clip Compiler

Turns a folder of game clips into compilations of your funniest moments, your best plays, or both. Google's Gemini watches and listens to each clip and scores what happens.

  • ~$0.0045per 30-second clip judged
  • Onceper clip: judgments are saved
  • Localyour clips are never modified

Gemini API key

Create a key in Google AI Studio and paste it here. It is stored in Windows Credential Manager, not in a file.

The free tier allows about 20 clips a day. Judging a real library needs billing turned on in AI Studio.

Where are your clips?

The folder your recorder saves replays to. Subfolders are included.
